About Unruly Labs LP

A two-principal operating company running an agent-fleet-orchestrated portfolio.

The pairing

Why two principals, one operating model.

Unruly Labs LP is a two-principal operating company. Ryan Dhookaran and Darsan Dinaram run the firm jointly, with Ryan also seated as Controller — the role that holds budget commitment, scope ratification, and locked-rule sign-off authority. The firm operates a portfolio of projects under one consolidated operating model: an agent-fleet-orchestrated build cadence in which both principals direct work across the portfolio rather than each owning a separate vertical.

The two principals bring distinct operating disciplines. Ryan brings a decade of product-management depth out of financial-services operating environments. Darsan brings five-plus years of enterprise IT systems administration, Microsoft 365 collaboration platform operations, and identity-and-security work — currently supporting a 100,000+ user healthcare environment as a Tier III Collaboration Analyst. The pairing is deliberate: one principal brings the product cycle and financial-services program-management governance; the other brings enterprise systems administration, collaboration-platform operations, and the troubleshooting discipline of a Tier II/III support engineer. Together they direct the firm's agent fleet across the portfolio.
