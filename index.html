How we work

Three steps. One week.

Most AI engagements end at the slide phase. Ours start at the deploy. Here is what happens between you sending an email and the build going live.

  1. 01 / Scope

    A 30-minute scope call.

    You send the URL or the problem statement. We respond inside a business day. The call runs 30 minutes and ends with a fixed-price proposal or a clear no.

    Day 1 · same week
  2. 02 / Build

    A 48-hour build sprint.

    The fleet builds against the scope while a principal audits each commit. You see progress in a shared channel, not a status deck. You get the production URL, not the staging one.

    Day 2 to 3
  3. 03 / Ship

    Production handoff.

    Live deploy, working code, working analytics, working docs. We hand you the keys. If something breaks in the first 30 days, we fix it on us.

    Day 3 to 5
