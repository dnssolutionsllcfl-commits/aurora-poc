## 1. The five pillars

**1.1 Receipts, not promises.** State what already shipped, not what we might do. "30 sites monitored live" beats "we monitor sites." "Scope call to public URL, same week" beats "fast turnaround."

**1.2 Outcome-language, not process-language.** Clients buy outcomes. Replace defensive negation ("no retainer creep") with positive promise ("scope locked at audit, ship public URL same week, receipts every Friday").

**1.3 Firm-level value-prop, never enumeration.** Peer pull (Stripe, Plaid, Linear, Modal, Anthropic, Vercel, Together.ai, Replicate) confirms: zero of eight count principals, agents, products, or LOBs in their hero. Neither do we.

**1.4 One business, multiple lines of business.** Unruly Labs LP is one operating company. The work it ships (Forge, Sentinel, Aurora, ARIA, internal portfolio) are LOBs inside one chassis, like a bank with checking and savings and loans. Never frame as N businesses.

**1.5 Operator-led, never agency-veneer.** Principals on every engagement. No account managers. No discovery phase. No retainer creep. The voice should sound like a senior operator who reads the firewall logs, not a sales brochure.

## 2. Banned phrases (Atlas-locked)

These never ship. Not in copy, not in alt text, not in og descriptions.

- leverage / comprehensive / seamless / robust / unlock
- harness / streamline / enable / synergy / drive
- "for the future" / "next decade" / "transforming"
- "industry-leading" / "best-in-class" / "world-class"
- "we are passionate about" / "we believe in"
- adversarial framings ("Your AI project is six months late")
- first-person CTA gimmicks ("Start my build" / "Get me started")
- day-anchor SLAs as gimmick ("48-hour guarantee")
- internal jargon visible to clients ("operator fleet" without explanation, "the fleet" as standalone CTA)

---

## 3. Banned punctuation (Atlas-locked)

**Em-dash is banned.** Use comma or period. Em-dash is an AI tell and a junior-copy tell. Period.

- Wrong: `principals on the call — agent fleet on the build`
- Right: `principals on the call, agent fleet on the build`

## 6. Voice quadrant (positioning against peer voices)

| Peer | Voice | Aurora picks up |
|---|---|---|
| Stripe | calm authority, benefit-as-fact | headline + sub-head rhythm |
| Linear | declarative, short, modern | tile titles, micro-headers |
| Plaid | outcome-led, warm | dual-CTA secondary ("Talk to a principal") |
| Anthropic | mission + identity | operator bios |
| Together.ai | quantified receipts | proof-row metrics |

Aurora's house blend: Stripe-clarity on the hero, Linear-modern on the tile titles, Plaid-warmth on the contact path, Together-receipts on the proof-row. Never copy whole sentences, copy the discipline.
